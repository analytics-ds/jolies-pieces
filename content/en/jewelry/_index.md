Designer jewelry is chosen for its design but judged by its material. This section gives you the benchmarks to read a product page, recognize a hallmark and understand what you are buying, then introduces the jewelry designers who matter.

## What you will find here

- **Materials** explained, solid gold, vermeil, gold plating, silver, pearls, and what French law says about them.
- **Hallmarks** and the details to look for before buying.
- **Jewelry designers** from France and Europe, their signatures and key pieces.
- **Gift ideas** by budget and occasion.
