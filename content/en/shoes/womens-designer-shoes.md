A designer shoe is recognised by its shape as much as by its label. A Repetto ballet flat, a Toral loafer or a K.Jacques sandal do not play in the same register, and are not necessarily bought in the same place. Online, you can go straight to the house or through a shop that selects several. This comparison brings together four specialist houses and a concept store, with the terms and prices recorded on 29 September 2026.

We deliberately left aside the big luxury platforms, already present in most rankings. No retailer has paid to be included.

## The specialist houses

### Repetto, the ballet flat since 1947

[Repetto](https://www.repetto.fr/) was born in 1947 and makes its ballet flats in France. It is the reference address for this model, with all its collections on its website.

### Bobbies, the clearest terms

[Bobbies](https://www.bobbies.com/), a Paris house, advertised on 29 September 2026 free relay-point delivery from 150 euros and free returns. A good choice if you are unsure about the size.

### Anthology Paris, shoes and bags

[Anthology Paris](https://www.anthology-paris.com/) offers shoes for women and men, as well as bags. Handy for putting together a complete look from a single house.

### Jonak, upmarket everyday

[Jonak](https://www.jonak.fr/) positions itself on upmarket women's footwear. Its delivery terms varied from page to page when we checked, so we do not quote them. Check them at checkout.

## The concept store, to compare

### Lulli sur la Toile

The [shoes section of Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/chaussures.html) brings together several designers, including Toral, Senso, K.Jacques and Repetto. The models recorded on 29 September 2026, all in stock, cover several families.

| Model at Lulli sur la Toile | Family | Price (29/09) |
|---|---|---|
| Toral, Kimana loafers | Loafer | 179 euros |
| Senso, Harriet III pumps | Pump | 210 euros |
| K.Jacques, Caravelle sandals | Sandal | 275 euros |
| Repetto, black Camille Ball ballet flats, made in France | Ballet flat | 330 euros |
| Repetto, white Zizi Oxford shoes | Oxford | 330 euros |

The point is to see side by side a loafer, a pump and a sandal from different designers, and to try them on in one of the company's fifteen or so boutiques. The [Repetto page at Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs/repetto.html) gathers the house's models available. The limit is the same as at any retailer, the choice of colours and sizes is narrower than on each house's own website.

## How to choose

- **You want a specific model.** The house's website will have every version.
- **You are unsure about the size.** Favour free returns, at Bobbies or Lulli sur la Toile, which grants them within 14 days.
- **You are looking for several styles.** A concept store saves you multiple orders.
- **You want made in France.** Repetto, direct or through a retailer.

## The limits of this comparison

We did not record prices on the houses' websites, as there was no equivalent model from one site to another. Jimmy Choo's website, often mentioned for this search, could not be consulted during our visit. Commercial terms change, especially during the sales. Lulli sur la Toile's prices are those of 29 September 2026.
