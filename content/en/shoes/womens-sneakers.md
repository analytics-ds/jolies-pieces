Women's sneakers are sold everywhere, and that is exactly what makes choosing difficult. On a brand's website, you get the whole catalogue and the house services. At a multi-brand shop, you see several labels side by side and can compare a Samba and a 1906 without switching tabs. This comparison sets three brand websites against a concept store that resells several, with the prices and terms recorded on 29 September 2026.

An important point, the prices quoted concern different models. They give an order of magnitude, not a ranking of the cheapest. No retailer has paid to be included.

## The comparison in one table

| Site | Type | What you will find | Prices recorded (29/09) | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Nike** | Brand website | The whole Nike catalogue | Depends on model | Free delivery and returns |
| **Veja** | Brand website | Sneakers in organic and recycled materials | Depends on model | Documented composition |
| **Golden Goose** | House website | Super-Star and the house's other models | Women's Super-Star 550 euros | Every version |
| **Lulli sur la Toile** | Multi-brand concept store | Golden Goose, New Balance, adidas, Nike, Autry, Asics | From 130 to 485 euros on four models | Several labels in one basket |

## The brand websites

### Nike, the full catalogue

On its [official website](https://www.nike.com/fr/), Nike advertises free delivery and returns. It is the most direct access to its entire range, including colours and sizes that retailers do not always carry.

### Veja, the sneaker that documents its making

[Veja](https://www.veja-store.com/fr_fr/) highlights organic cotton, Amazonian rubber and recycled materials in the composition of its models. For anyone who wants an everyday white sneaker and to know what it is made of, it is the most transparent address in this comparison.

### Golden Goose, the Super-Star at the source

The [Golden Goose website](https://www.goldengoose.com/fr/fr/) presents every version of its models. On 29 September 2026, the women's Super-Star was listed there at 550 euros. It is where you will find the most variants, at the price set by the house.

## The multi-brand shop, to compare

### Lulli sur la Toile

The [women's sneakers section of Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/chaussures/baskets-femme.html) brings together Golden Goose, New Balance, adidas, Nike, Autry and Asics. The four models recorded on 29 September 2026, all in stock, show the range.

| Model at Lulli sur la Toile | Price (29/09) |
|---|---|
| adidas Samba Rich Green | 130 euros |
| New Balance 1906 Moonbeam | 160 euros |
| Autry Windscape Low Cracklam | 225 euros |
| Golden Goose Super-star, white and pale pink leather | 485 euros |

The advantage is being able to compare very different silhouettes, from retro running to Golden Goose's leather sneaker, and to try them on in one of the company's fifteen or so boutiques. The [Golden Goose page at Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs/golden-goose.html) gathers the Italian house's models available. The limit is depth, a retailer only carries part of each brand's colours and sizes.

## How to choose

- **You have a specific model in mind.** The brand's website will have the full range.
- **You are torn between several styles.** A multi-brand shop saves you opening one account per brand.
- **Composition matters.** Veja documents its materials.
- **Returns reassure you.** Nike advertises free delivery and returns, Lulli sur la Toile free delivery from 150 euros and free returns within 14 days.

## What this comparison does not say

The New Balance and adidas websites could not be consulted during our check, so we quote none of their terms. The prices recorded concern distinct models and do not show that one site is cheaper than another. The Super-Star sold at 550 euros by Golden Goose and the one at 485 euros at Lulli sur la Toile are not the same version. Finally, trends move fast, the models quoted are those available that day.
