Buying branded clothing online involves a first choice, often an implicit one. Either you go straight to the website of a house you know, or you go through a shop that brings several together. Both make sense. The official site gives access to the whole collection and the house's services. The concept store lets you compare cuts and designers without opening six tabs.

This comparison brings together five French women's ready-to-wear houses selling direct and one multi-brand concept store. Prices were recorded on one piece per site and terms read on the delivery pages, on 29 September 2026. No retailer has paid to appear here.

## The houses' official websites

### Sandro, the collection and second-hand

[Sandro's website](https://www.sandro-paris.com/fr/) presents the autumn-winter 2026 collection and, alongside it, a second-hand offer. It is an interesting way in for anyone who wants the house without paying the price of a new piece, provided you accept a choice that depends on the items resold.

### Maje, the most complete terms

On 29 September 2026, [Maje](https://fr.maje.com/) advertised free delivery, exchanges and returns. It is the site in the panel that removes the most barriers to ordering. On price, a short checked dress was listed at 295 euros and wide-leg jeans at 235 euros.

### ba&sh, free delivery

[ba&sh](https://ba-sh.com/fr/fr/) offers free delivery. The nalo jacket was listed at 325 euros when we checked, the highest price among the official sites in this comparison.

### Rouje, Jeanne Damas's wardrobe

Founded by Jeanne Damas, [Rouje](https://www.rouje.com/) offers free delivery from 250 euros and split payment. The threshold is higher than elsewhere, which encourages you to group purchases.

### Sessùn, payment in three instalments

[Sessùn](https://www.sessun.com/) lets you pay in 3 interest-free instalments from 300 euros. Useful for a coat or a pricier seasonal piece.

## The concept store, to compare several designers

### Lulli sur la Toile

The [clothing section of Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/vetements.html) gathers European and international designers, with nearly 220 labels according to the company. The point is to compare different houses in a single basket, and to try things on in one of the network's fifteen or so boutiques. On 29 September 2026, a Forte_Forte grain de poudre skirt was listed at 420 euros and the Romane jacket from the [house label Jeanne Vouland](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs/jeanne-vouland.html) at 395 euros, both in stock. Free delivery from 150 euros, free returns within 14 days.

The trade-off is less depth for each designer. A concept store only offers part of the collections it selects, whereas the official site has everything.

## How to choose

- **You know which house you like.** Go to its official website, you will have every size and the whole collection.
- **You are torn between several styles.** A concept store saves you multiple orders and fees.
- **Returns worry you.** Maje, then Lulli sur la Toile, had the clearest terms at the time of our check.
- **You are aiming for an expensive piece.** Sessùn and Rouje offer split payment.
- **You want to shop differently.** Sandro's second-hand offer lets you buy the house without buying new.

## The limits of this comparison

We could not record a price for every site, as no comparable piece was displayed when we visited. The prices quoted concern different pieces and do not show that one site is cheaper than another. Commercial terms change often, especially as the sales approach. Finally, five houses do not cover all of French women's ready-to-wear, many others also sell direct.
