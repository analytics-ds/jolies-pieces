Designer ready-to-wear is clothing designed by someone you can name, cut in standard sizes and produced in small runs. This section looks at pieces up close, the cut, the fabric, the workshop, to help tell what is well made from what only looks it.

## What you will find here

- **The definitions** that matter, ready-to-wear, haute couture, designer, limited run.
- **The key pieces** of a wardrobe, the blazer, the shirt, the jeans, the dress, the trench, and what makes a good one.
- **Fabrics** and how they change the way a piece wears.
- **Designers** worth knowing, profiles and benchmarks.
