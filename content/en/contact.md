The editorial team reads every message and answers each one.

- **Report an error or request a correction.** Tell us which story, and if possible the source that supports the correction.
- **Introduce us to a designer or a store.** Describe the work in a few lines, with a link to the website.

To reach the editors, write to **redaction@jolies-pieces.fr**.

Jolies Pièces does not publish sponsored content. Paid placement proposals are not followed up.
