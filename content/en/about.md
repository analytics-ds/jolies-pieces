Jolies Pièces is an independent magazine devoted to fashion designers. We write for people who want to understand what they buy, what they wear, and what sets a carefully made piece apart from one that only looks the part.

## Our editorial line

We start from the piece. Its cut, its fabric, the workshop that made it, the designer who drew it. Then we look at where to find it, at what price, and what it is worth in daily use. Five sections structure the magazine, ready-to-wear, jewelry, shoes, bags and concept stores.

## Our method

- **Verifiable facts.** Materials, prices, origins, addresses and regulations are checked at the source before publication. When something cannot be verified, we do not write it.
- **Cited sources.** Every story lists its sources at the end of the page, with a direct link when the source is public.
- **Dated, updated stories.** The last update date appears on every page. A story that is no longer accurate gets corrected.
- **Selections that name everyone.** Our selections include every serious option, including those that outperform the others on a given point.

## Our independence

Jolies Pièces publishes **no sponsored content**, uses **no affiliate links** and sells **no placement** in its selections.
