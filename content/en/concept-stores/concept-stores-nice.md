In Nice, many shops call themselves concept stores, and they look very little alike. You will come across a designer wardrobe, a Scandinavian furniture showroom, a record shop that also sells furniture and a shop where you print your own t-shirt. This guide does not rank them. It groups them into three families, fashion, home and the unusual, so you know which door to push depending on what you are after.

Descriptions are taken from official websites, ratings, review volumes and opening hours from Google Maps, all consulted on 29 September 2026. No shop mentioned has paid for its place in this guide.

## Fashion

### Lulli sur la Toile, rue de la Liberté

Born in Marseille under Anne Vouland's lead, [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/toutes-nos-boutiques-mode/lulli-nice) now has around fifteen boutiques and nearly 220 labels according to the company. Its Nice address, 200 m² at 5 rue de la Liberté near place Masséna, opens Monday to Saturday from 10am to 7pm, and online orders can be collected there. Ganni, Jérôme Dreyfuss, Forte_Forte, Kujten, Ginette NY and Barbour are among the names shown in store. The Google rating, 4.5, rests on 48 reviews.

### Hattori, rue Catherine Segurane

In the port district, [Hattori](https://hattoriboutique.com/) presents itself as a concept store dedicated to womenswear, between minimalism and character. The selection brings together independent designers and premium labels, chosen for the quality of their materials and the precision of their cuts, in clothing, sneakers and accessories. The online shop offers, for example, a wide-leg jean at 195 euros. Open on Monday afternoons and from Tuesday to Saturday, with a midday break.

## Home

### Les Choses qu'on aime, rue Defly

[Les Choses qu'on aime](https://leschosesquonaime.fr/) celebrates a taste for beautiful things, art, craft and well-made objects. You will find homeware and small pieces for the house, such as an orange blossom scented candle sold for 25 euros on its website. It is one of the best-rated addresses in this guide, 4.9 from 79 reviews.

### Good Design Store, rue Catherine Segurane

[Good Design Store](http://www.good-designstore.com/) is dedicated to Scandinavian design furniture, lighting and Nordic decor, with houses such as Ferm Living, Muuto, Ethnicraft or Serax. The shop has several showrooms, including the one in Nice, and an online store. Its rating of 3.8 from 54 reviews is the lowest in the guide.

## Something different

### Pop Art, rue Saint-François de Paule

A few steps from the cours Saleya, [Pop Art](https://popart.fr/) combines a shop of clothing to customise with a gallery of artists' work. You design your own t-shirt, and the online shop advertises free delivery with no minimum order. With 145 Google reviews and a rating of 4.9, it is the most reviewed address in this guide.

### Evrlst, rue du Lycée

Evrlst is at once a record shop, a furniture store and a vintage clothing shop, listed as such on Google Maps. Its records are also sold online on a specialist platform. The shop opens Tuesday to Saturday, from midday.

## Where to start

- **To get dressed.** Lulli sur la Toile if you want a wide designer choice, leather goods and jewellery included, Hattori for a tighter selection around independent designers.
- **For the home.** Les Choses qu'on aime for objects and craft, Good Design Store for furniture.
- **To give something different.** Pop Art for a customised piece, Evrlst for a record or a second-hand find.
- **To buy without travelling.** Five addresses out of six have an online shop with a basket.

## What this guide does not say

This guide makes no claim to be exhaustive. ICI Concept Store, Trésors Publics, Mon Œil, La Baraque or Kosmos, though regularly mentioned, were set aside so that each category stays readable. Opening hours for Hattori and Evrlst come from Google Maps and may vary with the seasons. As for Google ratings, they capture a moment, and an average over 19 reviews is not as solid as one over 145.
