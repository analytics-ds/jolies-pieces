Looking for a designer multi-brand site often means looking for a trustworthy seller more than for a product. The same pair of shoes or the same jacket can turn up at several retailers, at the same price. What changes is the selection around it, the style of the site, where the parcel ships from and whether or not you can try things on somewhere. This comparison puts side by side five online shops that do not target the same customer.

To avoid repeating the most widely quoted rankings, we deliberately set aside the largest luxury platforms and the big Paris department stores. Delivery and returns terms were read on each site on 29 September 2026. No retailer has paid to be included.

## The comparison at a glance

| Site | Style | Origin | Strength | Ideal for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Antonioli** | Avant-garde luxury | Italy | A sharp, uncompromising selection | A bold, fashion-forward piece |
| **Browns** | International designers and home | United Kingdom | Fashion and objects together | A designer look and a beautiful object |
| **Centre Commercial** | Committed fashion | France | Ethical brands, free delivery from 100 euros | A responsible wardrobe |
| **Smallable** | Family concept store | France | Fashion, design and beauty for all the family | Dressing children and parents at once |
| **Lulli sur la Toile** | Women's designer wardrobe | France | Nearly 220 labels and boutiques to try on | Smart everyday dressing, with fitting possible |

## Antonioli, avant-garde luxury

[Antonioli](https://www.antonioli.eu/) is an Italian retailer whose selection embraces an avant-garde style, far from classic luxury. It is a site for those who already know what they want and like strong cuts. Orders ship from abroad, so read the delivery times and returns terms before confirming.

**Worth remembering.** A connoisseur's address, less suited to a first designer purchase.

## Browns, designers and home

[Browns](https://www.brownsfashion.com/) offers designer fashion and a home range. As the retailer is British, an order shipped from the United Kingdom may incur import VAT and duties. Depending on the case they are included in the displayed price or not, and the mention appears at checkout.

**Worth remembering.** A wide choice of designers, provided you check the import costs.

## Centre Commercial, committed fashion

[Centre Commercial](https://www.centrecommercial.cc/) presents itself as a committed concept store and selects ethical brands. On 29 September 2026, delivery was free in France from 100 euros, the lowest threshold in this comparison. The style is that of a sober, lasting wardrobe rather than the spectacular piece.

**Worth remembering.** The right reflex if the origin and making of your clothes matter as much as the cut.

## Smallable, the whole family

[Smallable](https://www.smallable.com/fr) defines itself as a family concept store. It offers fashion, design and beauty, for children as well as adults. It is the site in this comparison with the broadest range, at the cost of a selection less focused on the women's wardrobe.

**Worth remembering.** Ideal for a single basket for the whole household.

## Lulli sur la Toile, the women's designer wardrobe

Founded by Anne Vouland, [Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs.html) brings together nearly 220 labels according to the company, with around fifteen boutiques where online orders can also be collected. The selection mixes European and international designers, such as Ganni, Forte_Forte or Jérôme Dreyfuss, with the house label Jeanne Vouland. On 29 September 2026, a Forte_Forte grain de poudre skirt was listed at 420 euros and Jeanne Vouland's Romane jacket at 395 euros, both in stock. Delivery is free from 150 euros and returns are free within 14 days.

**Worth remembering.** The most practical site if you want to try things on in a boutique, though less cutting-edge than the two luxury addresses in this comparison.

## How to choose

- **You want a radical piece.** Antonioli, then Browns for a wider choice.
- **How clothes are made matters to you.** Centre Commercial.
- **You are buying for several people.** Smallable.
- **You want to try before you commit.** The [wardrobe of Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/vetements.html), thanks to its boutiques.
- **You are ordering from outside the European Union.** Read the duties and taxes line before paying.

## What each site does less well

None of these online shops is perfect. Antonioli and Browns ship from abroad, which lengthens delivery times and can complicate a return. Centre Commercial stays sober, not really suited to an evening outfit. Smallable spreads its choice across several audiences. Lulli sur la Toile, finally, focuses on the everyday women's designer wardrobe and does not compete with pure luxury selections on the biggest names.

## Our method

The sites were consulted on 29 September 2026. We only kept information readable on public pages, positioning, delivery thresholds, returns terms. The prices quoted at Lulli sur la Toile were recorded product by product, with their availability. Commercial terms change often, check them when you order.
