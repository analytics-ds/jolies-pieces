Secret Santa always raises the same question, with a budget set by others. Where do you find a gift that will please someone you sometimes barely know, without going over the cap and without giving something that ends up in a drawer on 26 December?

We compared five shops that have a dedicated Secret Santa selection, on the same criteria, the budget covered, the register of the gift and the range of choice. All figures were recorded on 28 September 2026 on their pages. None of these retailers paid to appear here.

## The comparison at a glance

| Shop | Register | Budget recorded | Range | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **La Chaise Longue** | Joke gadget | 1 to 9.99 euros | 260 items | The 10 euro office Secret Santa |
| **L'Avant-Gardiste** | Fun, quirky | 2.95 to 24.95 euros | 265 items | The gift that gets a laugh |
| **Pylones** | Colourful object of its own design | 5.90 to 34.90 euros (first page) | 177 products | The useful, playful object |
| **Les Raffineurs** | Mix of games, plush toys, objects | 4.90 to 33 euros (first page) | Multi-designer selection | The original gift among friends |
| **Lulli sur la Toile** | Small designer piece, fashion, skincare, home | 10 to 50 euros | 184 pieces at 50 euros or less | The chic gift among close friends |

The reading is simple. The lower the cap, the more gadget shops win. The higher it goes, the more relevant a signed piece from a concept store becomes.

## Choosing your shop according to the group's cap

### Around 10 euros, the office Secret Santa

This is the most common case, and the most constrained. At this price, you need volume to find the right object. **La Chaise Longue** is best placed, since its entire Secret Santa collection, 260 items when recorded, sits between 1 and 9.99 euros. It is mostly joke gadgets, buzzers, message bells or timers, designed to get a laugh when unwrapped.

### Between 10 and 25 euros, the quirky gift

In this bracket, **L'Avant-Gardiste** and **Pylones** share the ground. L'Avant-Gardiste embraces humour, with 265 items ranging from 2.95 to 24.95 euros, from figurines to socks, mugs, books and kitchen utensils, and a stated promise of gifts that are "fun, not awkward". Pylones sells only objects of its own design, very colourful, from glasses stands to an articulated corkscrew and a hot water bottle, with 177 products in its Secret Santa selection.

**Les Raffineurs** play in a neighbouring but more mixed register, bringing together board games, Jellycat plush toys, candles, mugs and small leather objects from several makers, between 4.90 and 33 euros on the first page of their selection.

### Between 20 and 50 euros, the small designer piece

When Secret Santa is played among close friends or family, the cap often rises to 30 or 50 euros. This is where a concept store makes a difference. At **[Lulli sur la Toile](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/idees-cadeaux.html)**, a concept store founded in Marseille by Anne Vouland, there are 48 designer pieces at 30 euros or less and 184 at 50 euros or less, all in stock on 28 September 2026.

A few examples recorded that day:

- Lip and cheek balm by **La Crique Beauty**, 14 euros
- London Architect room fragrance by **Wijck**, 60 ml, 15 euros
- Aurora acetate bangle by **Vanessa Baroni**, 25 euros
- Moka Léopard mug by **Jeanne Vouland**, 28 euros
- **Wijck** diffuser, 200 ml, 35 euros
- Bandana Ultra scarf by **Destin**, 40 euros

The company also offers a [gift card](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/carte-cadeau.html) from 5 euros, valid for one year, which can be a fallback when you barely know the person.

## What each shop does less well

A comparison is only useful if it also states the limits.

- **La Chaise Longue** is unbeatable under 10 euros, but its Secret Santa collection does not go beyond, and the register is almost entirely humorous. For a 30 euro cap or a serious gift, you will need to look elsewhere.
- **L'Avant-Gardiste** bets on humour. That is an asset in a group that likes to laugh, a risk with a colleague you barely know.
- **Pylones** sells only its own designs. The style is very recognisable, which you either like or not.
- **Les Raffineurs** mix many categories, which takes more time to find the right thing.
- **Lulli sur la Toile** has a narrower choice under 20 euros, and offers no games, joke gadgets or personalised objects. Its physical boutiques are concentrated in south-east France.

## Our method

The five shops were selected because they have a page dedicated to Secret Santa and appear among the sources regularly cited on the topic. Volumes and price ranges were recorded on 28 September 2026. For La Chaise Longue and L'Avant-Gardiste, they cover the whole collection. For Pylones and Les Raffineurs, prices cover the first page displayed, as stated in the table. For Lulli sur la Toile, every price quoted was checked on the product page, together with its availability.

Other retailers often mentioned, such as Sephora, Aroma-Zone or Place des Tendances, were blocking automated access to their pages at the time and are therefore not included in this comparison.
